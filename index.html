Lain Dain Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Lain Dain is a personal and business money-tracking application for recording amounts you owe, amounts owed to you, sales, purchases, payments, parties, due dates and related notes. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Lain Dain.

1. Information you provide

Lain Dain may store information that you choose to enter, including:

  • Your email address and account identifier.
  • Names of people, customers or suppliers that you add.
  • Debt, payment, sale, purchase and other money-record details.
  • Amounts, currencies, dates, due dates, categories and notes.
  • Your selected app mode and other app preferences.

2. Account and authentication

Lain Dain uses Firebase Authentication to provide account creation, sign-in, email verification and password reset. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google and Firebase process the information required to authenticate your account. Lain Dain does not receive or store your Google password.

3. Cloud data storage

Your Lain Dain account data is stored using Google Firebase Firestore so that your records can be synchronized across sessions and devices. Firestore may also keep local cached copies on your device to support offline use and synchronize pending changes when a connection becomes available.

4. Notifications and reminders

Lain Dain may use Android notifications and device scheduling features for due-date reminders. Notification permission can be controlled through your device settings.

5. How information is used

Your information is used only to operate the app, including to:

  • Authenticate your account.
  • Store and synchronize your Lain Dain records.
  • Calculate balances and display transaction history.
  • Remember your app preferences.
  • Provide due-date reminders.
  • Maintain app security and reliability.

6. Sharing of information

Lain Dain does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, including Google Firebase and Google Sign-In, subject to their own privacy terms and security practices. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

7. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect app data, including authenticated access to user-specific Firebase data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so you should also protect access to your device and account credentials.

8. Data retention and deletion

Lain Dain keeps account and app records for as long as they are needed to provide the service or until they are deleted. If account-deletion controls are added to the app or required by the distribution platform, this policy will be updated to describe that process. You may also use the developer contact information shown on Lain Dain's Google Play listing for privacy or deletion requests.

9. Children's privacy

Lain Dain is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact the developer using the Google Play listing.

10. Third-party services

The app currently relies on services that may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase Firestore
  • Google Sign-In / Google Identity services

These services may process technical information according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when Lain Dain adds, removes or changes features or third-party services. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, use the developer contact information shown on the official Lain Dain Google Play listing.
