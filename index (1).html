Lain Dain Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Lain Dain is a personal and business record-keeping application designed to help users track money they owe, money owed to them, business receivables, business payables, due dates, payments, and related records.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Lain Dain, we may collect and store information that you provide directly, including:

  • Your email address and account identifier.
  • Your selected app preferences, such as usage mode and default currency.
  • Names of people, customers, suppliers, or other parties that you enter.
  • Transaction and business record information, including amounts, currencies, payment history, notes, categories, and due dates.
  • Optional phone numbers that you enter or select when using the payment reminder feature.

This information is used to provide Lain Dain's core functionality and to synchronize your records across supported devices.

2. Account Information and Authentication

Lain Dain uses Firebase Authentication to provide account creation, email/password sign-in, email verification, password reset, and Google Sign-In.

Authentication information is processed through Firebase services operated by Google.

3. Cloud Storage

Lain Dain stores user records using Google Firebase Firestore.

This may include:

  • Account preferences.
  • Personal transactions.
  • Business entries.
  • Names associated with transactions or business records.
  • Notes and due dates.
  • Optional phone numbers saved for payment reminders.

Records are associated with the authenticated user's account and are protected using Firestore security rules intended to restrict users to their own data.

4. Payment Reminder Feature

Lain Dain allows users to prepare payment reminders through WhatsApp or SMS.

When a user chooses or enters a phone number for a payment reminder, that phone number may be saved with the associated transaction or business record in Firebase Firestore so it can be reused for future reminders.

Lain Dain does not automatically send messages on the user's behalf. The user reviews and sends the message through the selected WhatsApp or SMS application.

When a phone number is selected using the Android contact picker, Lain Dain only receives the contact information explicitly selected by the user. The app does not request unrestricted access to the user's entire contact list.

5. Notifications

Lain Dain uses local Android notifications to remind users about due payments.

Lain Dain also uses OneSignal for push-notification functionality. OneSignal may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, device information, notification subscription information, and app interaction information in order to provide push-notification services.

For more information, see the OneSignal Privacy Policy .

6. Advertising

Lain Dain displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information including:

  • IP address.
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address.
  • App interactions.
  • Diagnostic information.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or account identifiers.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Where required, Lain Dain uses Google's User Messaging Platform to provide advertising privacy and consent choices.

7. Data Sharing

Lain Dain does not sell users' personal financial records.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary for the app to operate, including:

  • Google Firebase for authentication and cloud database services.
  • Google AdMob for advertising.
  • OneSignal for push notifications.

Advertising and notification services may collect or process certain device, interaction, diagnostic, advertising, and notification-related information according to their own privacy practices.

8. Data Security

Lain Dain uses authentication and Firestore access controls intended to restrict access to each user's own records.

Data transmitted between the app and supported cloud services is sent using encrypted network connections provided by those services.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

User records remain associated with the user's account until they are deleted by the user or the account is deleted.

Lain Dain provides an in-app account deletion feature.

When account deletion completes, Lain Dain attempts to delete:

  • Personal transactions.
  • Business entries.
  • User profile information.
  • The associated Firebase Authentication account.

Users may also view account deletion information at:

https://waleed123e.github.io/LainDain-Privacy/delete-account/

10. Children's Privacy

Lain Dain is not specifically designed for children.

Users should only provide information that they are legally permitted to store and manage.

11. Third-Party Services

Lain Dain relies on third-party services including Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play Services, and OneSignal.

These services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Lain Dain's features or data practices change.

The latest version will be published at:

https://waleed123e.github.io/LainDain-Privacy/

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, users may contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Lain Dain Google Play listing.
