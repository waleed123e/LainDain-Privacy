Delete Your Lain Dain Account

Lain Dain lets you permanently delete your account and associated app data. Account deletion cannot be undone.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Lain Dain.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your identity when asked.
  5. Confirm permanent deletion.

The app deletes your Lain Dain user profile, personal transaction records, business entry records and Firebase Authentication account.

If you cannot access the app

Open the official Lain Dain Google Play listing and use the Developer contact information shown there to request account deletion. Send the request from the email address associated with your Lain Dain account so the request can be verified.

Open the Lain Dain Google Play listing

Data deleted

  • Your Lain Dain account profile.
  • Personal debt, lending and payment records.
  • Business sales, purchases and payment records.
  • Associated due-date records stored in your Lain Dain account.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Local device data

Temporary cached data may remain on a device until Android clears the app's local storage or the app is removed. Scheduled Lain Dain reminders are cancelled when deletion is completed in the app.
